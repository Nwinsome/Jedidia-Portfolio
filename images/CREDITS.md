## Preparation

Downloaded directly from Pexels' image CDN, requesting compressed WebP at quality
78, sRGB, and a bounded crop: 960 × 720 for the hero and 960 × 540 for both cards.
The site serves these saved files locally; no remote image request is needed.
The hero is prioritized; interest images are lazy-loaded and decoded asynchronously.
Explicit image dimensions reserve layout space.

Download URL pattern:
`https://images.pexels.com/photos/PHOTO_ID/pexels-photo-PHOTO_ID.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=960&h=HEIGHT&fit=crop&fm=webp&q=78`

Photo IDs, in table order: 20259607, 30144082, 29190138.
Geological contour and engineering line graphics in the page are original SVG/CSS artwork.
