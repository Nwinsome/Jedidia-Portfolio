↗   From Nsoatre, Ghana

My name is Darko Jedidia Agyeiwaa, a Level 100 Petroleum Engineering student at the University of Energy and Natural Resources (UENR), Ghana.

I come from Nsoatre in the Sunyani West Municipality of the Bono Region. I am passionate about the oil and gas industry, particularly petroleum exploration and drilling. I am eager to understand how petroleum resources are discovered, extracted, and managed efficiently and responsibly.

Beyond engineering, I am developing my skills in HTML, JavaScript, and digital marketing. As an aspiring petroleum engineer, my goal is to gain practical experience, strengthen my technical abilities, and contribute positively to the energy industry.
